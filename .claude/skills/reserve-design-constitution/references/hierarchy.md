# Hierarchy

Every screen must identify:

1. The single job of the screen
2. The information the athlete needs first
3. The primary action
4. Supporting information that may remain collapsed
5. Information that does not belong on the screen

Rules:

- Do not give every module equal weight.
- Do not solve complexity by adding more cards.
- Use typography, spacing and composition before borders.
- Use progressive disclosure for advanced data.
- Keep the primary decision visually unmistakable.
- Allow quiet space around important information.
- Remove repeated summaries that do not change the decision.
