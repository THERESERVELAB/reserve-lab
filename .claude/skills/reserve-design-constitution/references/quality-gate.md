# Quality Gate

Before approving a Reserve screen, answer:

## Purpose

- Is the screen's single job obvious?
- Is the primary action obvious?
- Was unnecessary information removed?

## Design

- Does the composition feel authored?
- Is hierarchy created without relying on card volume?
- Does typography carry the visual system?
- Is spacing deliberate?

## Product

- Does the screen use real member state?
- Are loading, empty, error, disabled and success states complete?
- Does the screen explain consequences before irreversible actions?

## Mobile

- Was the screen truly recomposed?
- Are touch targets and safe areas correct?
- Is the primary action reachable?
- Is there any horizontal overflow?

## Accessibility

- Is keyboard use complete?
- Is focus visible?
- Is semantic structure correct?
- Is reduced motion supported?
- Are contrast and text sizes appropriate?

## Final Question

Would a private premium performance company proudly feature this exact screen in its launch campaign?

If the answer is no, it is not finished.
