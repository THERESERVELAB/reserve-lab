# Brand

Reserve should feel:

- private
- intelligent
- composed
- precise
- exclusive without arrogance
- athletic without aggression
- clinical without sterility
- luxurious without decoration for decoration's sake

Reference qualities may be drawn from Equinox, Aman, Oura, Linear, Ferrari and Porsche configurators, but their assets or visual identities must not be copied.

Never connect Reserve publicly to:

- a founder
- an owner
- a creator identity
- a personal routine
- a founder's split
- celebrity-led fitness branding

Reserve speaks as an institution.
